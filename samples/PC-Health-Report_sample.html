What needs attention

CriticalBrowserRepairable (2 steps)

Chrome has a forced policy from "unknown-publisher"

A policy at HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\Google\Chrome\ sets a different homepage and a different search engine, and locks them so you cannot change them from Settings.

Why it matters: This is what Chrome shows as "Managed by your organization" and is the usual sign of a browser hijacker.

What to do: Delete the policy key, then set your real homepage and default search in Chrome.

Evidence (3)
  • HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\Google\Chrome\HomepageLocation = https://search-newsworld[.]com/
  • HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\Google\Chrome\DefaultSearchProviderEnabled = 1
  • HKLM\SOFTWARE\Policies\Google\Chrome\DefaultSearchProviderSearchURL = https://search-newsworld[.]com/?q={searchTerms}

CriticalMalware & PUPsRepairable (1 step)

PC App Store (Fast Corporation) adware is installed

All of its unique parts are present: C:\Users\Sam\PCAppStore\, PCAppStore.exe is running, four scheduled tasks named PCAppStore / PcAppStoreUpdater / Watchdog, and startup entries under Run.

Why it matters: Pop-ups, forced sign-up pages, slower start-up, and it reinstalls itself through Watchdog if you only uninstall part of it.

What to do: Open the Repair Console (RUN-REPAIRS.cmd) and choose "Remove PC App Store completely". It ends the programs, quarantines the folders, and removes the startup entries. Every step can be undone.

Evidence (5)
  • C:\Users\Sam\PCAppStore\PCAppStore.exe (running, PID 7412)
  • C:\Users\Sam\PCAppStore\PcAppStoreSRV.exe
  • Task \PCAppStore (runs at logon)
  • Task \Watchdog (runs every 10 minutes)
  • HKCU\...\Run\PCAppStore

HighAntivirusRepairable (2 steps)

Microsoft Defender has an exclusion for the whole C:\ drive

Folder exclusion: C:\. With this in place, Defender scans nothing on your Windows drive.

Why it matters: This is almost always set by malware or a crack to hide itself. Normal programs do not do this.

What to do: Remove the exclusion, then run a full Defender scan.

Evidence (1)
  • Preferences\ExclusionPath -> C:\
