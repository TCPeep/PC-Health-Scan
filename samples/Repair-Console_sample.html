How this works. Simulate shows exactly what a fix would do without changing anything. Apply does it with a live log. Repairs run one at a time; clicking several just queues them. Every change can be undone: an applied fix gets an Undo this fix button, and Undo & restore (top) lists every change ever made on this PC - including earlier sessions - with its own Undo button. Removed files go to a protected quarantine instead of being deleted, settings and registry keys are backed up first, and one System Restore point is made per session as a last resort. Steps marked caution remove or disable something. Steps that start unticked are ones where the item may well be legitimate - only tick them if you know it is unwanted. guided steps only open the right settings page.

Undo & restore

Newest first. Undoing a change puts back exactly what was there before it (registry values, tasks, services, files from quarantine, firewall rules, DNS, Defender settings...). Uninstalls and deleted temp files cannot be reversed here - use Open System Restore for those.
